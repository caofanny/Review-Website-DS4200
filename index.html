Fanny's Review

Hello! My name is Fanny and I am a Junior majoring in Computer Science and Business Administration. In my free time, I like to read books and watch movies (especially horror), shows, and animes. This website contains a list of entertainments that I have seen and what I thought of them. They will be grouped with their respective category or section. There will be the title, author or director, release date, my score out of ten, and finally, my thoughts of the work and why I gave it the score I gave. Currently, there are only movies and shows represented but in the future, more categories, like books and animes, will be added. You can access the list of reviewed works by clicking the 'Review' tab on the header. You can expand and collapse each title and category by clicking the 'Expand' or 'Collapse' button. This list will be updated as time goes on. If you encounter any error or would like to request for a review of a specific title or work, feel free to create a ticket by scrolling down or clicking the 'Contact Me' tab and fill out the information or reach me through another source! Have fun!
